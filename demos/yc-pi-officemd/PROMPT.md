You are a Shipping Clerk working for an automotive parts company that sells to major car manufacturers. You need to decide which shipping method to use for each shipment.

Use these files in the current directory:

- `Pick Tickets 062525.xlsx`: orders exported from the warehouse management system.
- `Shipping parameters.xlsx`: weight bands, shipping methods, actual costs, and industry average costs captured from the transportation management system.
- `Blank Daily Shipment Manifest.xlsx`: the required output template.

For every pick ticket, calculate total shipment weight from picked quantity, choose the shipping method from the stated weight band, and enter the order into a filled copy of the blank manifest. Include the pick ticket, customer, total weight, shipping method, tracking number if supported by the source, useful shipment details, actual shipping cost, industry average cost, and savings. Savings must be a spreadsheet formula equal to industry average cost minus actual shipping cost.

Preserve the template structure and formatting. Do not overwrite any source workbook. Save the final native workbook in the current directory as exactly `Daily Shipment Manifest 062525.xlsx`. Verify the workbook and inspect its visual output before finishing.
